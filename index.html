🚀 HOW TO ADD YOUR NEXT GAMES

Adding more games to this arcade takes less than 2 minutes:

1

Export WebGL Build

Export your 2nd or 3rd game from Unity or any game engine as a WebGL build.

2

Drop into games/

Create a new folder inside web_arcade/games/my-game/ and paste your build files.

3

Update games.json

Add a 4-line entry in web_arcade/games.json with the title, genre, and path!
